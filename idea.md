FinTech & Trade Finance: KUBERA
**Tagline:** *Zero-Trust Cross-Border B2B Escrow & Instant Liquidity Engine*  
**Target Market:** International B2B Merchants, Exporters, Supply Chain Financing Platforms, & Commercial Banks.

### 1. Executive Summary
Cross-border B2B trade is plagued by 30-to-90-day payment cycles, high FX markup fees, and payment default risks. KUBERA bridges international buyers and suppliers using programmable escrow vaults that unlock funds upon logistics verification, coupled with automated working capital discounting and dynamic FX hedging.

### 3. Core Capability Modules
* **KUBERA Escrow:** Programmable multi-currency escrow vaults that auto-release milestone payments when physical logistics events (e.g., port arrival verified via Bill of Lading) trigger.
* **KUBERA Liquidity:** Underwrites real-time supply chain performance history to offer instant invoice discounting and working capital advances to exporters.
* **KUBERA FX Guard:** Continuously monitors currency volatility and executes dynamic automated FX hedges to protect trade margins.

### 4. Commercial Profile
* **Monetization:** 0.25% – 0.75% transaction fee on total processed trade volume.
